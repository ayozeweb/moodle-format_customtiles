# Code of conduct / Código de conducta

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
and the spirit of the Moodle community guidelines.

In short: be kind and patient, assume good intent, criticise ideas and not people, and keep personal data
(yours or anyone else's, students' above all) out of issues, screenshots and logs.

Harassment, insults or discriminatory language are not accepted in issues, pull requests or any other space of the
project. The maintainer may edit or remove such content and block whoever posts it.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer privately through GitHub (the profile of
[@ayozeweb](https://github.com/ayozeweb)) instead of replying in public. Reports are handled confidentially.

---

contacta en privado con el mantenedor a través de GitHub.
